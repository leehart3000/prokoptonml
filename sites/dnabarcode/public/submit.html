What can I submit?

DNAbarcode.org identifies animals from the standard animal DNA barcode: a stretch of the mitochondrial COI gene (also written CO1 or COX1).

Accepted

  • One DNA sequence, pasted as FASTA (a line starting with >, then the sequence) or as plain letters.
  • 100 to 20,000 bases. Under 300 bases works, but answers are less certain. A standard barcode is about 650 bases.
  • Longer sequences that contain COI, such as a whole mitochondrial genome. The COI barcode region is found automatically.
  • Either strand. If you paste the reverse complement, it is turned round for you.
  • A few ambiguous bases (N, R, Y and so on), up to 10% of the sequence.

Not accepted

Privacy

Sequences are used only to answer your request. They are held briefly in the service's memory (so a repeated request is answered quickly), never saved to disk, and never written to our logs.
