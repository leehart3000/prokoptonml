Identify an animal from its DNA barcode

Paste a COI sequence. You get a cautious answer, explained step by step: a species where our data can tell, or a broader group where it can't.

Species-level answers are for mosquitoes (family Culicidae) for now. Other animals get a broad group. Scope and limits
